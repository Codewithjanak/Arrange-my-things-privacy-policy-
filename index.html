Privacy Policy for Arrange My Thing

Effective Date: October 20, 2026

Final Crew Games ("we," "our," or "us") operates the mobile game Arrange My Thing (the "Game"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle user information when you play our Game.

By downloading, installing, or using Arrange My Thing, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Do Not Collect Directly

We respect your privacy. Final Crew Games does not directly collect, store, or require any personally identifiable information (PII) from players, such as your real name, physical address, email address, phone number, or login credentials.

2. Information Collected Automatically by Third-Party Services

While we do not directly collect personal data, Arrange My Thing integrates third-party services that may automatically collect non-personal device information and technical data to deliver personalized or contextual advertisements.

Third-Party Service Providers:

  • Unity Ads
    • Purpose: To serve video and display advertisements within the Game.
    • Data Collected: Device type, operating system, network connection, hardware specifications, IP address, device location (country level), and advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID on Android).
    • For details on how Unity processes data, please review Unity’s Privacy Policy.

3. Children's Privacy

Because Arrange My Thing is directed toward a general audience including children under the age of 13, we comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play’s Families Policy:

  • We do not knowingly request or collect personally identifiable information from children.
  • Third-party ad networks (Unity Ads) are configured to serve age-appropriate advertisements in compliance with applicable child privacy laws.

If a parent or guardian becomes aware that their child has provided us with personal information without consent, please contact us immediately at finalcrewgames@gmail.com, and we will delete such information from our records.

4. In-App Purchases & Payments

Arrange My Thing does not feature any in-app purchases or paid microtransactions.

5. Security

We value your trust in providing us access to your device environment to play the Game. While no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, we rely on industry-standard practices provided by Unity Engine and Unity Ads to safeguard data.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be effective immediately upon updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any updates.
